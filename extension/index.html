Help

How to use Colorflower

Pick a color

  • Petals — click any petal to select its color.
  • Hue wheel — drag around the outer rainbow ring to set the hue.
  • Shade arc — drag the thick arc on the right to go lighter ⇄ darker; the handle shows your exact shade.
  • Precision — use the Hue and Saturation sliders for fine control.

The flower (pistil & bud)

  • Click the center pistil to slowly fold the flower into a bud; click it again to bloom open.
  • Toggle Bloom button hides/reopens the whole flower.
  • Bloom speed slider controls the open/close animation duration.

Generate palettes

  • Palette logic — Full spectrum, Complementary, Triadic, Analogous, Monochrome, Split-complementary, or Image.
  • Brand mood — biases saturation/lightness (Balanced, Luxury, Calm, Playful, Clinical, Editorial, Nature).
  • Locks — keep hue, saturation, or lightness fixed while regenerating.
  • Regenerate builds a fresh palette from the current logic, mood, and locks.

Read & check

  • Live HEX / RGB / HSL / OKLCH / OKLab values and a generated color name.
  • Contrast — type a background color to see the WCAG grade.
  • Compare — set color A and B to compare harmony and contrast.

Save & export

  • Bouquet — add colors to your saved palette.
  • Copy the selection as HEX, or export the bouquet as CSS tokens or JSON.
  • Image to flower — upload an image and the petals become its sampled colors.

Layout

  • Focus (top-right) collapses the side panel and top bar so the flower is centered; click again to expand.
